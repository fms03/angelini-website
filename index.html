Dr. Nicolas Angelini

Postdoctoral Mathematics Researcher

Currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Department of Mathematics, University of Trento, Italy. My research focuses on Geometric Measure Theory, Fractal Geometry, and the study of Intermediate Dimensions and Projections.
